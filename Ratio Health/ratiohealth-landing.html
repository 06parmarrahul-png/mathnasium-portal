The problem we're solving

Canadian healthcare runs on scheduling. Most of it isn't working.

Vacancy rates, float-pool failures, and last-minute overtime aren't a staffing problem alone — they're a scheduling-system problem. Tools designed for retail or generic enterprise workforces can't model seniority lists, can't respect collective agreement constraints, and can't verify a credential at the moment a shift is assigned. So managers do it on paper, spreadsheets, and phone calls — and clinicians burn out filling the gaps.

Ratio Health is built to sit inside the constraints Canadian health authorities actually work within — not around them.

What's different

Built for the constraints, not in spite of them.

Four design principles, baked in from the first line of code — not bolted on after an RFP.

Seniority-aware assignment

Vacancy fill, overtime offers, and shift posting follow seniority — modelled on HEABC NBA Article 25, not retail logic. Master lists are exportable and audit-postable.

Credential gating

Integrates with BCCNM, CPSBC, and the BC Care Aide Registry. An expired credential can't be scheduled — full stop. Renewal alerts fire to staff and managers automatically.

Self-scheduling with guardrails

Staff pick shifts inside collective agreement boundaries — fatigue rules, max consecutive shifts, premium thresholds. The system enforces the agreement so managers don't have to.

Audit-grade logging

Every schedule change, every override, every login. Immutable trail for grievances, FOI requests, and PIA evidence — without manager paperwork.

Layer, don't replace

Ratio Health can operate as the optimization and vacancy-fill layer on top of existing core scheduling systems. We're built to integrate, not to rip and replace.

Single-tenant deployment

Dedicated infrastructure available for health authority deployments. SAML SSO, Azure AD integration, and role-based access included as standard.

Compliance posture

Transparent about what we have and what's in progress.

Healthcare buyers deserve clarity, not certification-washing. Here's exactly where we are.

In place today

  • Canadian data residency
    All personal information stored and accessed within Canada (AWS ca-central-1).
  • PIPEDA & BC PIPA compliance
    Privacy policy, data-processing agreement, and sub-processor disclosure available on request.
  • Encryption at rest & in transit
    AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit. Key management via AWS KMS.
  • SAML / Azure AD SSO
    Federated authentication with role-based access control.
  • PIA support documentation
    Template Privacy Impact Assessment package available to accelerate FOIPPA s.69(5) review.

In progress

  • SOC 2 Type II
    Readiness underway. Observation window targeted to close within the next 12 months.
  • ISO 27001 + ISO 27799
    Healthcare-specific information security management certification on the roadmap following SOC 2.
  • Independent STRA
    Security Threat & Risk Assessment available for any deployment requiring a Statement of Acceptable Risk.
  • BC Bid & Bids and Tenders registration
    Active on BC Bid and the Fraser Health vendor portal for solicitation visibility.
